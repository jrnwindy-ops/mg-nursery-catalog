Tango Mandarin
Citrus reticulata | UC Riverside Release | Mid–Late Season | Patent Expired 2025 — Royalty-Free
Whole fruit
Cross section
Late Jan – Apr
HARVEST WINDOW
22 – 222 lbs
YIELD PER TREE
<1 seed/fruit
SEED CONTENT
Fruit Characteristics
  • Size: 59 mm (2.32") diameter, 48 mm height
  • Weight: ~90 g (3.2 oz) per fruit
  • Shape: Deeply oblate, no neck
  • Rind color: Deep orange (RHS N25A) — very smooth
  • Flesh color: Deep orange-red, finely textured
  • Juice content: ~50% — abundant and juicy
  • Flavor: Rich, sweet, distinctive
  • Segments: 9–10 per fruit
  • Seeds: <1 per fruit (0.22 avg) even in mixed plantings
  • Peel: Easy to peel; thin rind (~3.0 mm)
  • Brix: 12.5% avg at maturity, ~14.4% by April
Harvest & Maturity
  • Matures late January in California's San Joaquin Valley
  • Peak harvest: February through March
  • Fruit holds quality on-tree through April
  • Storage: excellent at 5.6°C for up to 60 days
  • Production begins year 2 — one year ahead of most mandarins
Yield Performance
  • Year 2 trees: 10–26 kg (22–57 lbs) per tree
  • Year 3 trees: 33–58 kg (73–128 lbs) per tree
  • Mature tree range: 90–101 kg (198–222 lbs) in peak years
  • Alternate bearing: Low — less than other late mandarins
  • Planting density: 150–250 trees/acre

  • Developed at UC Riverside (released 2006) from irradiated W. Murcott budwood
  • Rootstock: Carrizo or C35 citrange — excellent compatibility
  • USDA Zones: 9–11; full sun, well-drained soil
  • Growth habit: Upright when young → spherical at maturity
  • Mature tree: ~3.7 m tall × 3.8 m wide
  • Sterile pollen: Won't seed neighboring blocks

Shiranui (Sumo)
Citrus reticulata hybrid | Ponkan × Kiyomi | Japanese Origin 1972 | Premium Specialty Market
Whole fruit
Cross section
Jan – Apr
HARVEST WINDOW
30 – 80 lbs
YIELD PER TREE
Variable
SEED CONTENT
Fruit Characteristics
  • Size: Large — 8–10 cm (3.1–3.9") diameter
  • Weight: ~280–340 g (10–12 oz) per fruit
  • Shape: Oblate with distinctive protruding "topknot"
  • Rind color: Deep orange-yellow, thick but loose-fitting
  • Flesh color: Deep orange, tender-crisp texture
  • Juice content: High — succulent and aqueous
  • Flavor: Exceptionally sweet (13–18° Brix)
  • Acidity: Below 1% citric acid
  • Seeds: Seedless when isolated; seedy if cross-pollinated
  • Peel: Very easy to peel — rind separates loosely from flesh
Harvest & Maturity
  • Matures January through April
  • Peak quality typically February through March
  • Limited commercial season — January through April only
  • Fruit develops sweetness in storage (post-harvest hold-out)
  • Cold-hardy to approximately 25°F
Yield Performance
  • Mature tree range: 30+ lbs per tree (14+ kg)
  • Premium pricing: Sumo Citrus® sells at $4–8/lb retail
  • Bearing age: 1–2 years after planting (precocious)
  • Production: Self-fertile, moderate productivity
  • Tree size: 4–8 ft mature — compact

Rootstock Reference Guide
